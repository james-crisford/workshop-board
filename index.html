Every day

Your day

  1. You start at 9am. First job every day: clear the printers and clean the build plates.
  2. Stuck, or can't find Freddy or James? Message the group chat. We will always answer.
  3. A print has failed? Take a photo and send it to the group chat.

Opening a delivery box

  1. Take everything out and check every corner. Small bags and paperwork hide in the flaps.
  2. Delivery notes and invoices go to James. Never bin paperwork.
  3. Flat-pack the box straight away: open the bottom flaps, cut the tape if needed, fold it flat.
  4. Put the flat box outside.where exactly: James to add
  5. When you leave, take every flat box to the outside recycling bin.

Unwrapping a spool

  1. The plastic film goes in the silver bin.
  2. If the silver bin is full, empty it into the outside recycling bin, then carry on.

Before you leave

  1. Leave every place better than you found it. Sweep the floor and clear the benches.
  2. All flat boxes are in the outside recycling, and the silver bin isn't full.
  3. Every task you did today is Finished on this page, with a note if anything was slow.

eBay how-to

The eBay SKU board

  1. The SKU board is on the TV. Turn it on by pressing and holding the button on the left side.
  2. Use the mouse to move around the board.

Sorting parts into SKUs

  1. Desupport every part first. No part goes into a drawer or envelope with support still on.
  2. Update the stock count every time you add parts to a SKU, by the number you added. Do it straight away, not later.where the count is kept: James to add
  3. Each SKU goes in its own drawer. If the drawer is full, use a packing envelope from the ones on the freezers: one SKU per envelope, with the SKU number written on it.
  4. Put the finished SKU envelopes on the freezer, neatly.

Packing an order

  1. Pack the most overdue order first.
  2. Match the part to the photo on the order. If it doesn't look the same, stop and ask.
  3. Count the parts by hand against "What you get".
  4. HOLD (red flag): don't pack it, tell James. CHECK (amber flag): read why, then pack carefully. It means more than one, several parts, look-alike sizes, a left or right part, international, Amazon or Etsy, or several items on one order (one parcel, one label).
  5. Pick the size:
    Large letter
    up to 750 g · 35.3 x 25 x 2.5 cm
    Small parcel
    up to 2 kg · 45 x 35 x 16 cm
    Bigger or heavier: stop and ask. There is no Medium.
  6. Packing materials and who buys the label.James to add

Changing spools

  1. Only change spools when Freddy or James has said which filament goes on which printer.
  2. Read the spool label: white PLA and white PETG look the same.
  3. Unload the old filament from the printer's screen before pulling it out.
  4. Unwrap the new spool. The film goes in the silver bin.
  5. Cut the filament end cleanly at an angle and feed it in until the printer grabs it.
  6. Check the printer shows the right material and colour for that slot.James to confirm
